Long Range: Sniper Challenge Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Long Range: Sniper Challenge is a single-player iPhone game. It is built to work entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, makes no network connections, and contains no analytics, advertising, in-app purchases, or third-party tracking or SDKs. We never receive any information about you.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings, personal-best scores, and equipment unlocks in a file inside the app's own storage on your iPhone. This file never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@931devshop.com.
