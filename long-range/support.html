Long Range: Sniper Challenge Support

Questions or problems? Email support@931devshop.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game runs completely offline.

Where are my scores and unlocks saved?

On your iPhone only, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it does not carry over to a new iPhone.

Found a bug or have a feature request?

Email support@931devshop.com with as much detail as you can: what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened. Screenshots help.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: nothing is collected, and everything stays on your device.
