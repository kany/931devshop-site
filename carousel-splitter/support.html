Questions or problems? Email support@931devshop.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I post the slides in the right order?

Save to Photos saves the slides in order (slide 1 first). When creating your carousel post, select them in that order. If you share files instead, they are numbered carousel-001, carousel-002, and so on.

Why do my slides not line up?

Make sure every slide is posted at the same aspect ratio you exported (1:1 or 4:5). If the posting app crops differently, choose the matching ratio in Carousel Splitter.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Settings (gear icon on the home screen) and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

What's included in Pro?

Up to 20 slides, exports above 1080 px, blurred backgrounds, custom export presets, batch splitting of up to 20 photos, and unlimited saved projects. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Where are my projects stored?

On your iPhone only, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes your projects. Slides you've saved to Photos are not affected.

Carousel Splitter doesn't appear when I tap Share in Photos.

Scroll the app row in the share sheet to the end and tap More, then turn on Carousel Splitter.
